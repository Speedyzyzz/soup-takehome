5. Surprises & Open Concerns

What Surprised Us: The magnitude of the FP32 vocabulary logit spike during cross-entropy loss was our biggest systems surprise. With Qwen2.5's 151,936-token vocabulary, the transient memory for FP16 logits, FP32 upcast, shift_logits, and backward loss gradient consumed 2.04 GiB (32.5% of total training peak). On an 8 GB consumer GPU, this single tensor causes an immediate OOM even with batch size 2 and gradient checkpointing active. Additionally, BitsAndBytes NF4 double quantization preserved an astounding 90.19% Top-1 next-token agreement with only +0.0874 perplexity penalty over the FP16 model.

What Still Concerns Us: Although the model achieved a 25.72% held-out perplexity reduction and 100% EOS termination, we are concerned about subtle multi-step reasoning degradation (catastrophic forgetting on GSM8k/HumanEval) and RLHF refusal boundary erosion. Parameter-efficient fine-tuning on benign conversational data alters self-attention routing in ways that standard in-domain perplexity checks cannot penalize. Before deploying to production, this artifact must pass a multi-task canary regression benchmark in CI.

6. AI Tools Usage & Verification Audit

We used an AI coding assistant to scaffold initial script boilerplate and report formatting, and manually verified every component: (1) Caught Wrong LoRA Parameter Count: The AI estimated 14.84M parameters assuming uniform projection dimensions; we derived the exact 18,464,768 parameter count by inspecting Qwen2.5's GQA 12Q/2KV head architecture ({ ext{inter}}=8960$ across 28 layers). (2) Caught Smoltalk Deduplication Bug: The AI deduplicated by first_user turn, which collapsed 2,260 conversations to 5 because almost every dialogue opens with 'Hi'; we rewrote the deduplicator to hash the entire multi-turn transcript and enforced hard sample assertions. (3) Caught Incompatible Warmup Flag: The AI wrote warmup_ratio=0.05 which crashed in Transformers v5; we replaced it with warmup_steps=18. (4) Derived Memory Math: Every byte allocation in the memory budget was calculated by hand from tensor shapes and verified against continuous 1 Hz nvidia-smi telemetry.
